## Camera
Front-facing A7 III, vintage manual ink illustration. Airy ivory/transparent body with delicate charcoal contour lines, sparse grip stipple, concentric lens outlines, small crimson reflection. No app-icon blocks or three-quarter view. Generated once then simplified through imagegen edit. Asset: assets/a7iii-vintage-front.png.

## Photo mask
Landscape grayscale luminance mask. Black exterior, solid white center protecting the face, pooled gray and white ink blooms and fine capillary diffusion around perimeter. Liquid irregular dissolve, no torn-paper serration or linear geometric feather. Asset: assets/portrait-ink-wash-mask.png.
