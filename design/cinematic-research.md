# Cinematic sizing and pacing

## Research and design references

These film findings inform the art direction. They do not establish a validated sizing formula for a website.

- [Tim J. Smith, The Attentional Theory of Cinematic Continuity (2012)](https://ualresearchonline.arts.ac.uk/id/eprint/21187/). [Full manuscript](https://ualresearchonline.arts.ac.uk/id/eprint/21187/2/6679.pdf). The account connects continuity with viewers' attention and expectations. Its examples move between establishing, medium and close views. Website interpretation: give each section a clear focal point and retain recognizable visual relationships while changing scale.
- [Rooney and Bálint, Watching More Closely (2018)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.02349/full). In a 136-participant study, close shots increased mental-state attribution for a sad character, with no corresponding neutral-character effect or general ability benefit. Scale works with content and context. [Corrigendum](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.00261/full): a reference-year correction, conclusions unchanged.
- [SavoirFaire, Naked City Films design and build case study (2026)](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/01/19/naked-city-films-designing-and-building-a-website-that-refuses-to-stand-still/). Firsthand design reference for dramatic scale, spare copy, varied compositions and editorial pacing. The portfolio borrows those principles while preserving its existing red, black and paper identity.
- [Original Khaled Mehran portfolio reference](https://www.behance.net/gallery/249911437/Khaled-Mehran-Portfolio-2026). Retain the childhood portrait, oversized local-script name, restrained side motifs and black About section.

## Applied decisions

| Area | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Cover | Centered composition with a larger, container-scaled Devanagari title. Accepted ink mask and photographic grain remain. |
| About | Larger name and face composition, short existing introduction, more breathing room. |
| Work | Novo and Herald use wide feature rows with alternating image positions. Other projects form a quieter two-column sequence. |
| Films | Large portrait and landscape frames, original source ratios, quiet metadata and prominent play controls. |
| Photography | Portrait pairs alternate with wide views. Images retain their proportions rather than receiving a cinema-ratio crop. |
| Honors and Contact | Strong display hierarchy, thinner supporting details, larger closing section. |
| Viewer | Pure black frame and dimmed surroundings focus attention on the selected work. |
| Motion | Gentle 0.8-second individual frame entrances. Reduced-motion preference disables them. |
| Compact screens | Feature rows stack, titles scale down, controls and content remain readable. |

The 1440px content width, 4.5–10rem scene spacing and responsive type sizes are design choices. Research does not prescribe these values.
